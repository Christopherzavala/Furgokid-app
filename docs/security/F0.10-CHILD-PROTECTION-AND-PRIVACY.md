## 1. Executive Summary

This document details the technical implementation of F0.10 controls for child protection and data privacy within Furgokid, adhering strictly to the privacy-by-design and privacy-by-default architecture.

---

## 2. Implemented Controls

1. **Adult-Only Accounts:** Minors are strictly prohibited from holding user accounts, credentials, or direct authentication sessions. All user accounts registered in Firebase Auth and Firestore `users` are strictly adult users (`role == 'parent'` or `role == 'driver'`).
2. **Authorized Passenger Onboarding:** Minor passengers are onboarded exclusively by authenticated adult parent users (`role == 'parent'`) who declare adult responsibility.
3. **Adult Responsibility Declaration:** When onboarding a passenger, the system records:
   - `responsibilityDeclared`: `true`
   - `purpose`: e.g., `'school_transport'`
   - `declaredAt`: server timestamp
   - `authorizationVersion`: `'v1.0'`
   - _Note:_ The system logs the adult's responsibility declaration without claiming that formal documentary legal guardianship was legally or officially verified by the platform.
4. **Explicit Authorization States:** Passenger authorizations follow a strict state machine: `pending`, `active`, `revoked`, `expired`, `rejected`.
5. **Passenger Data Minimization:** Passenger records only store minimal non-identifiable identifiers (`passengerId`, `parentId`, `passengerAlias`, `status`, timestamps). Collecting full birth dates, phone numbers, emails, schools, photographs, health data, or special needs by default is prohibited and blocked by backend validation (`DISALLOWED_PASSENGER_FIELDS`).
6. **Retirement of `specialNeeds`:** The `specialNeeds` field has been retired from new flows and contracts. Historical records are preserved without automatic destructive migration.
7. **GPS Limited to Current Location:** Real-time tracking coordinates are restricted exclusively to `matches/{matchId}/tracking/current`. No historical GPS tracking points or logs are stored in legacy history collections. Revocation, logout, and match closure/cancellation immediately trigger location cleanup and purge active tracking documents.
8. **Logging & Telemetry Hygiene:** Sentry and logging utilities are sanitized to strip raw GPS coordinates (`latitude`, `longitude`), child names, tokens, and PII from error reports and application logs.
9. **AdMob Disabled for Minor Transport:** AdMob advertising is disabled (`adsMode: 'disabled'`) for minor transport flows and child passenger screens.
10. **Generic Push Notifications:** Push notification payloads are strictly generic (e.g., "Tu servicio de transporte escolar se ha actualizado") and contain zero child PII or GPS coordinates.
11. **Fail-Closed Security Rules & Backend Validation:** Firestore Security Rules (`firestore.rules`) and Cloud Functions enforce fail-closed authorization checks for all passenger and tracking collections.
12. **Separation of Adult & Passenger Data:** Adult user profiles reside in `users/`, whereas minor passenger metadata resides in separate `passengers/` and `passengerAuthorizations/` collections.

## 3. Technical-Normative Analysis: Ley 21.719 (Chile)

_Disclaimer: This analysis is technical and informational only and does not constitute formal legal advice or legal certification of compliance._

- **Context:** Ley 21.719 updates Chilean data protection regulations, emphasizing minor data protection, explicit adult consent, data minimization, and purpose limitation.
- **Implementation Alignment:** Furgokid implements privacy-by-design by minimizing minor data collection, requiring explicit adult responsibility declarations, enforcing strict purpose limitations (`school_transport`), and providing revocation and location cleanup mechanisms.
- **Pending Review:** Formal compliance certification requires independent professional legal review and formal legal counsel sign-off.
